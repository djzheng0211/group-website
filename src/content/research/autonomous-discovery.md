## Motivation

The vast range of material compositions, synthesis conditions, and operating
parameters makes systematic exploration challenging, while dynamic structures
and competing reaction mechanisms complicate efforts to establish clear
structure–performance relationships and understand the underlying science.
We combine automated experimentation,
modeling, and agentic AI to identify promising materials and test
mechanistic hypotheses, aiming to accelerate discovery while revealing the
principles that guide material design.

## Approach

We integrate automated instruments, computational models, and AI agents to
connect synthesis, characterization, and testing in an iterative discovery
process. Each round builds on accumulated evidence, adapting the platform to
different materials classes, scientific questions, and performance targets.

- **Synthesis:** automated preparation of composition and processing libraries
- **Measurement:** rapid screening with integrated characterization, tailored
  to relevant properties such as activity and selectivity for catalysts or
  ionic conductivity and stability for membranes
- **Modeling:** statistical models, machine learning, and physical simulations
  that connect composition and processing with material properties, test
  mechanistic hypotheses, and guide experimental design
- **Agentic AI:** agents that translate research goals into experimental plans,
  coordinate instruments and analysis tools, interpret results, and refine
  hypotheses to guide subsequent experiments
